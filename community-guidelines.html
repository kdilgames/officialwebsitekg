Community Guidelines

Rules and expectations for participating in KDILGAMES community spaces.

Last Updated: May 15, 2026

Respectful Behavior

All community members are expected to treat each other with respect. We do not tolerate:

  • Harassment, discrimination, or hate speech based on race, ethnicity, gender, religion, sexual orientation, disability, or any other protected characteristic
  • Threats, intimidation, or personal attacks
  • Doxxing or sharing others' personal information without consent

Inappropriate Content

The following content is strictly prohibited in all community spaces:

  • NSFW (Not Safe For Work) or adult content of any kind
  • Illegal content or promotion of illegal activities
  • Spam, scams, phishing, or fraudulent content
  • Excessive profanity or disruptive behavior

Product Rules

To protect our work and the community, the following are strictly prohibited:

  • Leaking, sharing, or distributing KDILGAMES paid products in any way
  • Reselling, redistributing, or claiming KDILGAMES products as your own
  • Assisting others in obtaining paid products without purchase

Exploitation & Cheating

Discussion of exploits, cheats, or hacking in a malicious or harmful way is not allowed. This includes promoting or distributing cheats, exploits, or methods to violate Roblox's terms of service or other platform rules.

Consequences

Staff reserve the right to take appropriate action for violations, including but not limited to:

  • Verbal or written warnings
  • Temporary or permanent mute from community channels
  • Temporary or permanent ban from community spaces
  • Revocation of product licenses and support access (for severe violations)

Contact & Reporting

To report violations or concerns, please contact us privately at:
